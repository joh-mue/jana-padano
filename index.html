Jana Padano is an amazing DJ, playing everything from tiny basement clubs to packed main floors. Once the first record drops, Jana gets entrenched in the music, lost in every groove and every beat. No playlists, no shortcuts: only the realest records make it into the crate.

Every set is a journey through sound, a sweaty, euphoric ritual that turns strangers into family. When the needle hits the wax, time stands still and the dancefloor becomes one living, breathing organism. Jana doesn't just play music. Jana makes the walls sweat and the sunrise jealous.
